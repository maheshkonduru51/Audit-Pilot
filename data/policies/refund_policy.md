# Refund Policy

## Refund window
Travel essentials and eligible physical goods may be returned within 10 days of delivery. Other service categories have a 30-day cancellation window unless a contract says otherwise. Damaged goods are eligible within the applicable window.

## Approval limits
Refunds up to Rs 2,000 within policy can be auto-approved. Refunds from Rs 2,001 to Rs 25,000 require manager approval. Refunds above Rs 25,000 or outside the applicable policy window require admin approval with a written reason.
